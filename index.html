CSV aur copy wali list hamesha upar nazar aa rahi mojooda scan ki banti hai — live scan ke baad wohi nayi list export hogi. Roz ki final scan (Mon–Fri, market close ke baad) WhatsApp report mein bhi aati rahegi.

Screener kaise kaam karta hai

  • BUY: EMA 9 (short) neechay se upar nikal jaye EMA 26 (long) ke — pehle EMA 9 neechay tha, ab upar hai.
  • SELL: EMA 9 upar se neechay cross kar jaye EMA 26 ko.
  • Cross pakarne ke liye live EMA values pichli saved values (baseline) se compare hoti hain — is liye sirf naye cross nazar aate hain, purane trend wale stocks nahi. Daily ki baseline 23 Nov 2026 ki hai; baqi timeframes mein pehli scan baseline banati hai aur cross agli scan se aata hai.
  • Signal tab pakka hota hai jab us timeframe ka bar close ho — Daily ka final signal 3:30 PM close ke baad.
