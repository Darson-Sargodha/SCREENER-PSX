# PSX EMA Screener

Live EMA 9 / EMA 26 crossover screener for all Pakistan Stock Exchange stocks.

Open the page, pick a timeframe (Daily, Weekly, 4H, 1H, 15m), tap Scan — it fetches live data from TradingView in the browser and lists BUY (EMA 9 crosses above EMA 26) and SELL (EMA 9 crosses below EMA 26) signals with price, % change, volume and EMA values.

Daily crosses are compared against the 23 Nov 2026 baseline. Other timeframes build their baseline on the first scan and show crosses from the next scan. Without internet it shows the saved 25 Nov 2026 scan.

Screener signals only — not investment advice.
